How to explain this in an interview

“I fixed the model and evaluation rules before opening the final test period. At each target hour, observed inputs stop two hours earlier. I compared the model and baselines on identical targets, and reported both average performance and cases where the uncertainty ranges failed.”

The model was fitted using labels through 31 December 2016 at 22:00. Earlier test-period observations may become lag inputs after the reporting delay, but they never retrain the model. This page replays saved predictions; it does not train or run live inference.
