Completed 14 October 2026 (Asia/Colombo). Today we built 15 inputs for the later models: the hour/day calendar, traffic counts from earlier hours and days, and a recent average. For a 10:00 forecast, the newest traffic reading allowed is 08:00. This protects the experiment from accidentally seeing answers too early.

Every historical value is matched by its timestamp. If a reading is missing, it stays missing; an actual zero stays zero.
