## In simple English

We finally tested on the period kept aside during model development. The tree's average absolute error was 185.6 vehicles/hour, compared with 272.9 for the weekday/hour baseline on the same 6,533 observed hours. That is about 32% less average error, not 32% accuracy. Its nominal 90% ranges covered 90.6% overall, but only 78.4% at 22:00. A good average can hide unreliable hours.

The demo lets you choose one of the first 48 test hours, see what information was available, compare the forecasts and reveal what actually happened. It is saved historical replay, not a live traffic system. The first 48 hours were chosen before viewing the test results, so they are not selected success stories.
