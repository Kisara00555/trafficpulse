# Part 6 - Check whether performance holds across time

Completed 17 October 2026. Today we checked four successive quarters instead of trusting one yearly average. We also compared keeping the original fitted model with refitting it every three months using only observations that would already be available.

The tree beat all three baselines and Ridge in every quarter under both policies. Its quarterly-refit average absolute error ranged from 175.3 to 206.4 vehicles/hour. Refitting barely changed yearly error: 190.16 without refitting versus 189.98 with it. That small difference is not enough to claim a reliable improvement or change our reference policy.

We exposed weaknesses too. Winter and weekend errors were higher, and the largest observed error was an overprediction of 3,792.8 vehicles/hour despite complete inputs. We do not know the cause from this analysis. A useful model can still make large mistakes; uncertainty ranges are the next part.

All 42 tests passed with no skips. Independent calculations matched the saved pooled and quarterly errors, a repeated full run reproduced the output hashes, and the fixed-origin results matched Part 5. Final holdout data remains sealed. See [the full report](../BACKTEST_RESULTS.md) for scope and limitations.
